# Creator market evidence for presentation statistics

Accessed: November 6, 2026.

Use **73% independently run** and **27% direct brand outreach** as survey percentages, with the sample qualifications below. The evidence does not establish an absolute number of solo creators or a current average number of inbound brand approaches per creator.

## 73%: creators handling their business independently

Source: [CreatorIQ × Influencers.club, *The State of Creators* (2026)](https://www.creatoriq.com/whitepaper/state-of-creators), Creator Businesses → business support; methodology under About this survey. Interactive chart also checked through [Agent Reach/Jina](https://r.jina.ai/https://www.creatoriq.com/whitepaper/state-of-creators).

- Exact question: “Who supports your business?”
- Response: **73%** selected “I handle everything independently”; **8%** selected “A talent manager or agent.”
- Sample/date: **5,095 creators across 100 countries**, fielded **May 29–June 29, 2026**; the question's displayed base is also n=5,095.
- Slide wording: **73% of surveyed creators handle their business independently.**
- Limit: self-reported business support, not a worldwide census of solo creators. The 8% management response does not make the remaining 92% solo businesses.

## 27%: direct outreach as a way to find brand deals

Source: [The Influencer Marketing Factory, *Creator Economy Report 2026*](https://theinfluencermarketingfactory.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Creator-Economy-Report-2026.pdf), **p. 18 methodology; p. 22 result** (PDF page numbers).

- Exact question: “How do you typically find brand deals?*”
- Response: **27%**, “Receiving direct brand outreach.” The asterisk denotes a **multi-select** question.
- Sample/date: **1,000 U.S. creators aged 18–65**, surveyed **January 2026**.
- Slide wording: **27% of surveyed U.S. creators report finding brand deals through direct brand outreach.**
- Limit: a reported sourcing channel, not brands or messages per month, deal share, or conversion rate. Respondents were not restricted to solo creators; this separate sample cannot establish outreach among CreatorIQ's independently run subset.

## Approximately 67 million: total creators, not solo creators

Source: [Goldman Sachs, *Creator Economy: Framing Market Opportunity* (March 26, 2025), pp. 1 and 4](https://creatorswithinfluence.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Goldman-Sachs-Global-Investment-Research-Creator-Economy-Framing-Market-Opportunity-Download-Report-March-26-2025.pdf) (Goldman-authored primary report hosted on a third-party site).

Goldman estimates **approximately 67 million total global creators in 2025**, drawing on earlier estimates and Zippia data. This is an analyst estimate, with no applicable survey question or respondent base for a solo-creator count. It is neither a count of independently run businesses nor a 2026 census. Do not multiply this estimate by another study's percentage to claim **49 million or 61 million solo creators**: the populations, definitions, and dates are not aligned.

## Older monthly-message evidence; no verified current universal average

Source: [HypeAuditor, *Influencer Outreach Survey*](https://blog.hypeauditor.com/influencer-outreach-survey/), outreach-frequency question and Methodology.

- Exact question: “How many messages do you receive on average each month from brands and agencies seeking to work with you?”
- Sample/date: **1,656 self-identified influencers worldwide with verified social accounts exceeding 1,000 followers**, surveyed online in **September 2021**. Published September 24, 2021; the displayed November 3, 2026 update does not change the survey vintage.
- Finding: **37% reported 1–3 messages per month**. This is a response interval for a subgroup, not an arithmetic mean across respondents or a count of unique brands. The sample is not restricted to solo creators.
- Assessment: unsuitable as a current average for the slide. Targeted searches in the preceding research found **no verified current universal average** of inbound brand approaches; that is a research limitation, not proof that none exists.
